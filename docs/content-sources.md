### Heat-pump explainer

[Xie et al. (2024), doi:10.1016/j.scs.2024.105811](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scs.2024.105811), *Sustainable Cities and Society* **115**, 105811; [institutional record and paper](https://researchportal.plymouth.ac.uk/en/publications/could-residential-air-source-heat-pumps-exacerbate-outdoor-summer/).

Use §3.1 and Figure 7 for the **example-day peak differences for idealised London flats**: summer changes of 19.3 W/m² and 0.12°C refer to 20 July; winter decreases of 11.1 W/m² and 0.16°C refer to 8 February. The abstract's “medians” wording differs from the detailed results. Do not recast these values as seasonal medians or study-wide maxima.

The reference uses gas heating without active summer cooling. The model uses sequential annual time-series coupling, 50% building plan-area coverage and post-1996 fabric. Inputs are UKCP09-derived Design Summer Years for 2040–2059 under the medium-emissions scenario; the nine-city analysis uses flats. Results are simulated neighbourhood conditions at 2 m, not measurements beside individual units. The [Zenodo files](https://zenodo.org/records/13351764) were restricted when checked on 8 November 2026; check again before claiming unrestricted access. Link [SUEWS](https://suews.io/) for the model itself.
