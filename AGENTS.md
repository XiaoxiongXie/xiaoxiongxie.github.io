# Project conventions

- Work in this repository, the source for `https://xiaoxiongxie.github.io/`. Do not create another repository or change hosting providers.
- Use Quarto, QMD, SCSS and small local scripts. Quarto supplies Deno and Pandoc Lua; no Node, Python, R or package installation is needed to build.
- Preserve the academic design, accessibility and responsive layouts. The owner authorised Cloudflare Web Analytics on 10 November 2026; this is the only permitted analytics provider. Add no other analytics, remote fonts, widgets or unlicensed images.
- Keep Cloudflare's public beacon token in `data/analytics.json` (null disables it); never store account credentials. Load the beacon only on the configured public site, after checking the visitor's opt-out and browser privacy preference. Local previews, error/redirect pages and the existing privacy page must not send analytics. Keep a short disclosure and browser-specific opt-out on the existing privacy page, with no new page or banner. The repository remains public; publication still requires the owner's request.
- Keep the navigation pinned. Home contains the concise profile, verified professional links, research focus and contact; no publication list. Research explains questions, methods and supporting papers; Projects contains only funded projects. Background contains experience, education, awards and service. Teaching retains the owner's five confirmed modules.
- Preserve `/about/` and the former `/projects/#cibse-award` bookmarks. Exclude the retired About page from search/sitemap.
- Configure the optional authorised portrait and verified professional links in `data/profile.json`; no placeholder photo or invented teaching details. Research paper cards use publication IDs through `generated-paper` QMD blocks and share the bibliography.
- Edit publication records in `data/publications.json`. Preserve verified full titles, author order, DOI, primary-source URL and journal issue year. Record date nuances in `docs/content-sources.md`.
- Publications has no Selected badges/filter. Research centres first-author work and labels collaborative studies clearly; do not infer corresponding authorship. Order teaching modules by code. Exeter lecture title, department and February 2026 date come from the owner's CV; no course is specified. Keep the footer personal: `Xiaoxiong Xie`.
- Keep CV additions selective: module periods, undergraduate supervision versus assisted MSc supervision, four invited talks and two related funded projects. Preserve CV role distinctions and participation dates; CIBSE AM11 work remains in progress. No News page is wanted at present.
- Verify academic facts. Do not invent grants, outputs, findings or credentials. UMAR is complete; display only `2025–2026` on Projects, without a status label or exact project dates. Keep its public entry to a brief project overview, PI role, funder, partner and official links. Keep private source documents, unpublished results, detailed project notes, financial tables and third-party contact details out of every tracked file and Git history, including documentation and comments.
- Keep modelling results distinct from observations and retain quantitative scope and limitations.
- Write concise research narratives: explain the question, approach and finding in connected prose. State material assumptions with the methods or results; avoid repeated disclaimers and audit language in public pages. Keep only public sources, bibliographic nuances and current image licences in `docs/content-sources.md`. Detailed verification and editorial records belong in ignored `.local/` storage, not public documentation.
- The monitoring theme pairs Xie et al. (2020), using fixed weather stations and building simulation, with Lam et al. (2025), using mobile monitoring. Credit the latter as a co-authored study led by Charlie Lam; do not present it as Xie's own project. Retain the `#thermal-exposure` bookmark.
- Keep icons sparse, local and decorative. Research uses one first-author result figure per theme. Figures require verified reuse rights, descriptive alt text, a full-size link and visible paper/license attribution. Preserve the 2020 monitoring and 2022 longwave accepted-manuscript figures' CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 terms and unchanged content; the 2023 ventilation and 2024 heat-pump figures use CC BY 4.0. Lam's collaboration is a short text entry with Charlie's link. Record provenance in `docs/content-sources.md`. No PDF extraction tools are needed to build the site.
- Use explicit heading IDs for cross-page links and QMD links in prose. Support a `site-url` with a repository subpath.
- Keep browser titles concise: page label plus Quarto's automatic site-name suffix (Home uses `Profile`). Research uses `Key results.` consistently, a full-size figure button before each image and a compact source/licence line. Link author/year/figure labels directly to the appropriate source record; keep manuscript versions in link titles and provenance notes rather than separate visible links. Preserve original aspect ratios and the local SVG connectors between Home's three themes.
- Use the navy-and-white XX favicon. Desktop Home connectors align with theme headings and have equal gaps; exclude heading-anchor buttons from the centred theme labels. Home invites PhD enquiries from self-funded or externally funded applicants and asks for the funding source; omit a separate negative studentship statement. Teaching links to that information. Publication source URLs remain in the records/schema; Research Portal and BibTeX are compact page-end links.
- `_generated`, `_site`, `.quarto`, `.tools` and `.local` are ignored local state. Never commit tools, downloaded papers, credentials, private backups or rendered output. Strip personal photo metadata before committing; preserve display orientation, image pixels and colour profiles. Ignored files need a separate private backup and do not survive a fresh clone.
- Anchor Quarto resource globs at the project root (`/assets/**`, `/robots.txt`); unanchored patterns also match nested local review copies. Keep the output-directory hygiene checks in the validator.
- Preserve the owner's 9 November 2026 preference: allow search and user-directed retrieval, opt out of documented AI training uses. Keep explicit training exclusions in `robots.txt` and Bing preferences in `includes/ai-preferences.html`; verify provider scope/rollout dates in `docs/search-discovery.md` before changing them. Google-Extended also restricts Gemini grounding. Bing's current noarchive setting restricts AI answers; separate training/grounding controls begin on 1 February 2027 with recrawling. Do not claim robots rules guarantee exclusion from all training or remove previous copies.
- Run `quarto render` and `quarto run scripts/validate.ts`; check changed interactions and mobile/desktop layouts. Test a project subpath after URL changes.
- Use official GitHub Pages actions; deploy only `main` after validation. Do not claim publication before a deployment succeeds.
- Publication was resumed at the owner's request on 9 November 2026. Keep push deployment disabled; each publication requires manual `workflow_dispatch` with `publish_site: true`. Deploy and run the workflow's search notification only when the owner requests publication of that update.
